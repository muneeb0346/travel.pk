Karachi to Dubai Flight Route Guide

Direct flights from Karachi (KHI) to Dubai (DXB) typically take about 2 hours 15 minutes. Pakistan is PKT (UTC+5) and the UAE is GST (UTC+4), so Dubai is one hour behind Karachi.

Airlines on this route

  • Pakistan International Airlines (PIA)
  • Emirates (EK)
  • FlyDubai (FZ)
  • Air Arabia (G9)
  • Qatar Airways (QR) via Doha

Typical fares

Economy fares start around PKR 54,200; business class from roughly PKR 88,000.
